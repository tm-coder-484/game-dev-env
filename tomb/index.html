✻ How to play
  • Swipe (or arrow keys, WASD, HJKL, a gamepad). Clawd slides until he hits a wall. You can't stop halfway.
  • Collect tokens (dots), coins and the three stars, then slide into the door at the top.
  • Spikes only hurt if you stop on them. Bugs and gates cross corridors: standing still is always safe, so wait for a gap.
  • Traps spring half a second after you land. The context window rises from below on some stages. Keep climbing.
  • Power-ups: shield (survive one hit), magnet (pulls in tokens), pause (stops every hazard for 5 s).
